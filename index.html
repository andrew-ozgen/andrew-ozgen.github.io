Embedded USB Music Player

May 2025

A USB music player on an STM32 with DAC audio output to external speakers. Interrupt-driven timing and double buffering in a multithreaded architecture keep USB reads and DAC output in sync for distortion-free playback.

LCD shows track name and elapsed time; an IR remote (NEC protocol) handles play, pause, skip, and rewind. Also implemented playlist management, auto-advance, long-filename handling, and an LED volume meter.
